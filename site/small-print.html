The terms

Last changed 17 November 2026

In short: be kind at the table, what you write stays yours, and we will tell you before anything here changes.

Who runs Poiesis

Poiesis (poetdaily.org) is run by Nikolos Kyritsopoulos, a private individual, as a free and non-commercial project. "We" and "us" on this page mean him. You can reach him at hello@poetdaily.org. By using Poiesis you agree to these terms.

Who may join

You must be 13 or older to make an account. If you are under 18, you need a parent's or guardian's okay first. If we learn that someone under 13 has made an account, we close it and delete what it holds.

Your account

One account per person. Keep your password to yourself; you are responsible for what is written from your account. Pick a username that does not pretend to be someone else.

Table manners

Poiesis is a place to aspire together, not to compete. At the table, please do not:

  • harass, threaten, mock or demean anyone, or write hatefully about any group;
  • post anything sexual involving a minor, or anything sexually explicit at all;
  • share someone else's private information;
  • post spam, advertising or links meant to sell something;
  • pass off someone else's writing as your own, or copy long passages of writing that is still under copyright (short quotations to make a point are fine);
  • break the law, or try to break, overload or get around the site's rules.

Your writing is yours

You keep all rights in what you write on Poiesis. To run the site, you give us a non-exclusive, free, worldwide permission to store, copy and show your writing, but only for these purposes: to show it to you, and, for what you post at the table, to show it to the members who can see that day's conversation. We will ask you, each time, before quoting your writing anywhere else, including in the daily letter. This permission ends when you delete your writing or your account (copies may stay in backups for up to 30 days).

When you keep a line of someone else's writing in your notebook, it is for your own notebook only. Please do not republish another member's writing without asking them.

When something goes wrong

If a post breaks these manners, use Report under it, or write to us. We may hide a post or close an account that breaks them. A hidden post is not destroyed: we keep it privately in case the decision was wrong and it should come back. You can delete your own posts at any time.

The daily letter

The letter is sent only if you say yes, and only for the letter. You can stop it from the footer of the site at any time, and every letter will carry a way to stop it.

What we promise, and what we cannot

Poiesis is a working sketch, offered free and as it is. We do our best to keep it running and to keep your writing safe, but we cannot promise it will always be available, free of mistakes, or kept forever; please keep your own copy of anything you cannot bear to lose. We may change or stop the site. As far as the law allows, we are not responsible for losses that come from using it. Nothing here takes away rights you have under the consumer laws of the place where you live.

Leaving, and changes

You can stop using Poiesis whenever you like; write to us and we will delete your account. If we change these terms in a way that matters, we will put the new date at the top and tell members before the change takes effect.

Your privacy

Last changed 17 November 2026

In short: we keep only what the site needs, we never sell it, there are no ads and no tracking, and much of what you write never leaves your own device.

Who is responsible

Nikolos Kyritsopoulos is responsible for your personal data on Poiesis (the "controller", in European law). Write to hello@poetdaily.org about anything on this page.

What stays on your own device

Your notebook pages, your questions, your colours and settings, and anything you write without an account are kept in your own browser's storage. They are not sent to us, and we cannot see them. Clearing your browser's site data erases them.

What we keep if you make an account

  • Your email address and password. Used only to let you sign in, and to write to you about your account. The password is stored scrambled (hashed); nobody can read it.
  • Your username. Shown to other members.
  • What you post at the table (first impressions, links and replies), and when you posted it.
  • What you save from your notebook (kept lines and kept writing), so it follows you to other devices. Only you can see it.
  • Your answer about the daily letter.

We also keep a small sign-in key in your browser's storage so that you stay signed in. It is needed for the site to work, so there is no cookie banner. We use no analytics, no advertising and no tracking of any kind.

Why we may use it (the legal basis)

To give you the account and the table you asked for (a contract with you); to send the letter (your consent, which you can take back at any time); and to keep the site safe and the table kind (our legitimate interest in running a safe place).

Who else handles it

We never sell or rent your data. A few services help run the site, and see only what they need:

  • Cloudflare hosts the site. Like any web host, it sees your internet address when you visit, to deliver the page and protect it from attacks.
  • Supabase runs the accounts and the database that hold the account information above.
  • Wikimedia Commons supplies the paintings. Your browser loads them from there, so Wikimedia sees your internet address.
  • FormSubmit or Google, only if you send us answers on the feedback page, to deliver them to us.
  • When the daily letter begins, it will go through an email-sending service, which we will name here before the first letter is sent.

Some of these services keep data on servers outside the country where you live, including in the United States. They do so under their own data-protection terms, which include the safeguards European law requires.

How long we keep it

For as long as you have an account. When you delete a post it is gone from the site. When you ask us to delete your account, we delete it and what it holds within 30 days, apart from any hidden post we must keep as a record of a moderation decision.

Your rights

You may ask to see the data we hold about you, to correct it, to have it deleted, to receive a copy to take elsewhere, or to object to how we use it. Write to us and we will answer within a month. If you think we have handled your data wrongly, you can also complain to the data-protection authority where you live (in Italy, the Garante per la protezione dei dati personali).

Young members

Poiesis is not for children under 13, and members under 18 need a parent's or guardian's okay. A parent or guardian may write to us to see or delete a young member's data.

The works and copyright

Last changed 17 November 2026

In short: the old works belong to everyone, the words around them belong to Poiesis, and if we have made a mistake we will fix it quickly.

The day's works

The poems, passages, Scripture and paintings shown on Poiesis were chosen because they are in the public domain in the United States: the texts were published before 1929, the Scripture is the World English Bible, and the paintings are old works reproduced from Wikimedia Commons. Each work carries its source. Some may still be under copyright in other countries; if you live elsewhere, please check before reusing them.

Poiesis's own words

The writing around the works — the essays, the stories of each muse, the notes, questions and translations — and the stickers and design of the site are © 2026 Nikolos Kyritsopoulos, all rights reserved. You are welcome to quote a little, with a link back.

The site's code is open source under the MIT licence; the typefaces are under the SIL Open Font Licence. Both licences are published with the code.

If you believe something here is yours

If you own the rights to something on Poiesis and did not agree to it being here, write to hello@poetdaily.org with:

  • what the work is, and where it appears on the site;
  • your name and how to reach you;
  • a statement that you believe in good faith its use here is not allowed, and that you own the rights or may act for the owner.

We will take it down promptly while we look into it. If it was a member's post, we will tell them; if they believe it was taken down by mistake, they may write to us and we will look again.
