# Letters to a Young Poet, 6
*Rainer Maria Rilke, Rome, 23 December 1903*

```text
Warum denken Sie nicht, daß er der Kommende ist, der von Ewigkeit her bevorsteht, der Zukünftige, die endliche Frucht eines Baumes, dessen Blätter wir sind?

Why don't you think of him as the one who is coming, who has been approaching from all eternity, the one who is to come, the final fruit of a tree whose leaves we are?
```

> Note: Short on purpose: one sentence of the spine. Translate the rest of letter 6 for the letter if needed.

## ⓘ The story
Written from Rome two days before Christmas 1903 to the 20-year-old cadet Franz Kappus, who was alone at a garrison. This season's spine book.

## Go deeper
What if the thing you've lost is actually still coming toward you?
