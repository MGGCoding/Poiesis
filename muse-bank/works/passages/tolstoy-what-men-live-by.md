# What Men Live By (the angel's last words)
*Leo Tolstoy, translated by Louise and Aylmer Maude, 1885; translation 1906*

```text
I have now understood that though it seems to men that they live by care for themselves, in truth it is love alone by which they live. He who has love, is in God, and God is in him, for God is love.
```

## ⓘ The story
A poor shoemaker takes in a naked stranger found by a shrine. After six years, the stranger turns out to be an angel sent to earth to learn three things. Tolstoy wrote it in 1885 for ordinary readers.

## Go deeper
What do you actually live by, underneath what you think you live by?
