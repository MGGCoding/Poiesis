# Les Misérables I.2.12: The Bishop Works
*Victor Hugo, translated by Isabel F. Hapgood, 1862; translation 1887*

```text
“Do not forget, never forget, that you have promised to use this money in becoming an honest man.” … “Jean Valjean, my brother, you no longer belong to evil, but to good. It is your soul that I buy from you; I withdraw it from black thoughts and the spirit of perdition, and I give it to God.”
```

## ⓘ The story
Jean Valjean, just out of prison, steals the Bishop of Digne's silver. When the police bring him back, the bishop says it was a gift and adds the candlesticks.

## Go deeper
When has generosity bought something back for you that you had given up on?
