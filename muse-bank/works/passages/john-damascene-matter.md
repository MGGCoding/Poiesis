# On Holy Images, I
*St John of Damascus, translated by Mary H. Allies, c. 730; translation 1898*

```text
I do not worship matter, I worship the God of matter, who became matter for my sake, and deigned to inhabit matter, who worked out my salvation through matter. I will not cease from honouring that matter which works my salvation. I venerate it, though not as God.
```

## ⓘ The story
John wrote from a monastery near Jerusalem, outside the emperor's reach, against the destruction of icons. The Seventh Ecumenical Council, remembered today, upheld his case in 787.
