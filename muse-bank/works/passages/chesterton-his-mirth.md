# Orthodoxy, ch. IX (last paragraph)
*G. K. Chesterton, 1908*

```text
Joy, which was the small publicity of the pagan, is the gigantic secret of the Christian. … I say it with reverence; there was in that shattering personality a thread that must be called shyness. There was something that He hid from all men when He went up a mountain to pray. There was something that He covered constantly by abrupt silence or impetuous isolation. There was some one thing that was too great for God to show us when He walked upon our earth; and I have sometimes fancied that it was His mirth.
```

## ⓘ The story
The last words of Orthodoxy. Chesterton wrote the book in answer to a critic who said he'd never explained what he himself believed.
