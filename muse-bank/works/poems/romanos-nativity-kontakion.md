# Kontakion of the Nativity (“Today the Virgin”)
*St Romanos the Melodist, 6th century*

```text
Ἡ Παρθένος σήμερον τὸν ὑπερούσιον τίκτει,
καὶ ἡ γῆ τὸ σπήλαιον τῷ ἀπροσίτῳ προσάγει·
Ἄγγελοι μετὰ ποιμένων δοξολογοῦσι·
Μάγοι δὲ μετὰ ἀστέρος ὁδοιποροῦσι·
δι' ἡμᾶς γὰρ ἐγεννήθη
Παιδίον νέον, ὁ πρὸ αἰώνων Θεός.

Today the Virgin gives birth to the One beyond all being,
and the earth offers a cave to the Unapproachable.
Angels sing glory with the shepherds;
Magi travel with a star;
for to us is born
a little Child, God before the ages.
```

## ⓘ The story
Tradition says Romanos, a deacon from Emesa who was mocked for his reading, fell asleep at a Christmas vigil around 518. The Theotokos gave him a scroll to eat, and the next morning he sang this. It is still sung at every Orthodox Christmas.
