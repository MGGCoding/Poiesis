# Songs of Kabîr, I (“O servant, where dost thou seek Me?”)
*Kabir, translated by Rabindranath Tagore, 15th century; translation 1915*

```text
O servant, where dost thou seek Me?
Lo! I am beside thee.
I am neither in temple nor in mosque: I am neither in Kaaba nor in Kailash:
Neither am I in rites and ceremonies, nor in Yoga and renunciation.
If thou art a true seeker, thou shalt at once see Me: thou shalt meet Me in a moment of time.
Kabîr says, "O Sadhu! God is the breath of all breath."
```

## ⓘ The story
Kabir was a 15th-century weaver of Benares, claimed by Hindus and Muslims alike. His songs were passed on orally for generations before they were written down.
