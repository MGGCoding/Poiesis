**Catholic:** The Holy Guardian Angels · Mass: Job 38:1, 12–21; 40:3–5; Psalm 139; Matthew 18:1–5, 10

## D · broken ✝
*Psalm 51 · Orthodox Matins and Compline; Friday Morning Prayer*

**Psalm 51:17** (World English Bible)

> The sacrifices of God are a broken spirit.
> O God, you will not despise a broken and contrite heart.

| | |
|---|---|
| Poem | [Love (III)](../works/poems/herbert-love-iii.md), George Herbert |
| Passage | [The Brothers Karamazov](../works/passages/dostoevsky-peasant-women.md), Fyodor Dostoevsky, translated by Constance Garnett |

**Why:** A broken and contrite heart God will not despise; Love bids the guilty guest sit down; Zosima: no sin the Lord will not forgive.

<sub>Where it falls: Psalm 51 (50 in the Greek numbering) is said every day at Orthodox Matins and Small Compline, and is the Friday Morning Prayer psalm in the Roman Hours (on 2 Oct Universalis gives the Guardian Angels' festal psalms instead).</sub>
