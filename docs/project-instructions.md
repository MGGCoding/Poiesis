# Poiesis: project instructions

## What Poiesis is
Poiesis is an app/website/online forum I'm developing. It is a space to communicate with a MUSE (an inspiration channeled through creative works), with others who share their thoughts, and with oneself. It is inspired by René Girard's idea of good mimesis: users all aspire to a shared model instead of competing with one another.

The origin is GS, a high-school group where we read a poem on the spot and talked about our personal experience of it, connected to a broad sense of God and the spiritual life (Mary Oliver–like). Poiesis tries to give that to people who don't have a GS.

## The five pillars (the test for every feature)
1. Works are meant to inspire a gift of imagination.
2. You should always be able to capture your thoughts, wherever you are in the app.
3. Conversations must always be visible, with the maximum incentive to contribute well.
4. Always creation over consumption.
5. The mystery of the muse cannot be scared off by optimization, envy or possession.

## Decisions already made (don't undo without asking)
- Two pages only, **Muse** and **Notebook**, with a switch in the center of the header. Profile is reached from the avatar; **Why** lives in the footer.
- The daily letter is an email, not a page on the site.
- **First impression** (never "seal"): the prompt is "How does this muse call to your own experience? What do you hear it singing?" with the placeholder "One sentence is enough." The writing box is always open beside the work.
- You must leave a first impression before reading anyone else's (the BeReal-style gate).
- Each muse gets **one first impression plus one later thought**. Others can mark "Moved me", write back, and bookmark to Journal, Scrapbook or an idea board.
- Conversations stay open for **3 days**, then close, keeping the top 10 impressions and up to 3 replies each.
- **Stickers**: one a day. Each first impression unlocks that muse's sticker; you keep one until midnight. Answer all of the day's muses and your sticker is gilded. Makers are repeatable series with new editions. The profile shows only stickers you've collected.
- **No public counts or leaderboards.** Only the maker sees who was moved.
- **Notebook**: season dial (Gathering shows everything; Tending, Weaving and Giving split into an always-open writing page plus a space you choose, with a left/right swap). Journal with import from Apple Notes files, "Copy for Notes", and a trash for torn-out pages. Pinterest-style idea boards with a corkboard view. Scrapbook. Rule of life with a private ledger.
- Hover definitions on hard words. Highlight any line to quote or pin it. A pen button on every page for quick notes.
- Rooms (color themes) and typefaces are independent. The defaults are the Study room and IM Fell English / Literata type.
- Faith framing is **implicit and ecumenical** in the product, for now.
- Never mention Revenue Recovery Co. in anything about Poiesis.

## Style
- Homey, handmade and personable: notecards, paper, handwriting. It should not look AI-generated or over-designed.
- Friction on purpose: easy to navigate, but built so people sit with each muse.
- Must work on phones.
- Paintings appear as labeled color studies until real images are licensed or hosted.
