# Poiesis: decisions log (through 22 Sept 2026)

Every answer given during the prototype rounds, oldest first. Later rounds override earlier ones where they conflict.

## Round 1: first prototype
- Site type: manifesto intro plus a working demo (later changed; see Round 4).
- Desktop/tablet first, still works on phones (later: must be fully mobile).
- Name: **Poiesis**, with each daily inspiration called "a muse."
- Faith framing: implicit / ecumenical.
- Feed friction: one muse at a time.
- Social: respond with your own making, written comments, pin to board.
- Creation framework: no strict stages. Instead, a **season** you toggle, which changes the app's emphasis.
- Accountability: a personal Rule of life plus one companion. Log-in metrics and uploads stay private and never in your face (tally-mark ledger).
- Season and theme are separate controls.
- Themes: Study/corkboard, Monastery, Roman terracotta, Night desk, Garden, Café, Library.

## Round 2
- Mission moved to a Why tab; the two definitions of poiesis stay at the top.
- Headline: "Poiesis, a place to make things" → "Every made thing began as a thing received."
- Theme button is an icon; type (fonts) is a separate picker, not tied to themes. Keep the current font as default.
- The daily email invites you in to read and journal.
- Pages move forward and backward through a small set of muses per day.
- First reaction is fixed, but you can add to it.
- Responses live under each muse; Rule of life moved into the Notebook.
- Notebook: journal, scrapbook and idea board as three separate spaces, easy to move between so nothing gets lost.
- Tried several "write beside it" methods across the muses (margin, pencil, one line that grows, highlight to quote, copy-it-out).
- Sealed first thought stays visible, with later layers underneath; others can like, comment and save it.
- Site opened on the Email tab (later removed).

## Round 3
- Stray note about Miltiades/Cimon: ignore.
- "Seal" → **"first impression."**
- Hover definitions on hard words only (curated glossary).
- Author edit mode: skipped for now.
- Seasons: Gathering shows everything; other seasons split with an always-open writing page, and you pick the other half.
- A pen on every page for quick notes.
- Curator/writer/moderator contact opens an email (placeholder address hello@poiesis.example until a real one is given).
- Profile: name, photo, a line about you, who formed you, badges/roles, stickers.
- Stickers collected from muses and shown on the profile; comments show the person's photo, top muse and a badge.

## Round 4: current prototype
- Stickers need scarcity: one sticker per day. Empty slots above the muses fill as you leave impressions; pick one before midnight. Makers are repeatable series (Keats, James, Mary Oliver–type) with new editions.
- First-impression prompt: "How does this muse call to your own experience? What do you hear it singing?" Always open, with "One sentence is enough."
- Apple Notes: import from exported files, plus copy/share back to Notes.
- Notebook side can switch left/right.
- Idea board: Pinterest-style boards plus a corkboard view.
- Trash bin keeps torn-out pages, except empty pages or title-only pages.
- Profile shows only obtained stickers, and each links back to its muse.
- No repeated commenting: **one impression plus one later thought**.
- Conversations last **3 days**, then close. The muse stays, with the top ~10 impressions and their top 3 replies.
- Letter page removed from the site and saved as a PDF.
- Why moved to the footer; only two pages, Muse and Notebook, with a center switch.
- Must be mobile compatible.

## Founder's notes (Round 5)
- Origin: GS (see the project instructions).
- Pitch: a common model of pursuit through positive mimesis of an unobtainable model; a common muse that inspires all. Guiding questions on first impressions so people don't type filler.
- "Twitter is organized threads"; Poiesis is organized dialogue with the muse, others and oneself.
- Incentives: BeReal-style gate, stickers, proper meditation, molding a muse with creative imagination, prompts.
- Dialogues should be saved; refresh to see all chats.
- Marketing: people respond to muses on TikTok/Instagram; publish notebook artifacts to Substack.
- Business: an interactive newsletter, funded like Substack (patrons / paid subscriptions).
- Muses can be art, poetry, passages, quotes or questions.
- Notebook artifacts: gather → reflect → intend → publish. Writers place muse stickers on their pages; readers rate how significant the reference was. (Flagged: conflicts with pillar 5.)
- Offline mode to read the muse and write.
- Synthesis as genius: muses come in themes, contrast with each other and span mediums. Maybe comments across multiple muses. See all works published on a muse.

## Pushback already raised (see founder's notes, section 9)

## Round 7 (22–23 Sept 2026): the Muse page, final
- **The triangle is dropped.** The day's three works (poem, passage, painting) sit in a row. Tapping one opens it.
- **Links stay, with the same rules:** a thread between two neighbours is the side link and opens once both are answered; an arch over the row (or a loop under it) joins poem & painting; a knot after the third is "all three" and opens once all three are answered.
- The hero headline and the poiesis definition leave the Muse page (they stay on Why). The story of each work sits behind an ⓘ by the title instead of under the work.
- The first-impression box is now a notebook that runs the length of the work (desktop). A drawing, wallpaper or wash fills the empty side of the poem. Line numbers, glossary marks and paper are adjustable.
- "N others wrote today" hangs as a pinned note beside the three.
- **Keeping a sticker:** after each first impression a card asks whether to keep that sticker today; you can swap until midnight (also from the profile's "Today's sticker" panel).
- **Settings** live in the profile (Your profile | Settings). Wide screens and phones keep separate settings; the house style is Niko's picks. Room and type pickers stay in the header too.
- Kept from before: Moved me, Write back, bookmarking, adding thoughts under your impression, links shown under every work they touch, highlight-to-quote, past muses, the quick-note pen.
- Workshop: "Muse Page Studio" artifact (https://claude.ai/artifact/2xt3BQeJvFNFi5GgyLNgib) holds the toggles used to choose these.

## Round 9 (24 Sept 2026): everything merged into the live prototype
The Muse page (Round 7), the Muse Curriculum (Round 8), the revised sticker mechanic and the Notebook Studio's round-2 notebook are now all in version 7 of the live artifact. Poiesis.html and this log were updated to match.

### The curriculum, in the product
- **Today's three come from the guide's day one, *familiar*** (Luke 4:22-30): Barrett Browning, *Aurora Leigh* VII ("Earth's crammed with heaven"); Chesterton, "Do it again" from *Orthodoxy* ch. IV; Hammershoi, *Interior in Strandgade, Sunlight on the Floor* (1901, SMK). All public domain, each with a sourced story. The Light day (Dickinson, Thoreau, Vermeer) moved to past muses.
- **The theme word is revealed after all three impressions on desktop too** (the phone already did this), which settles the open question from Round 8. A small cross sits beside the revealed word; tapping it opens the day's verse (World English Bible) with the reading reference and a line saying the liturgy shapes the day but is not its point.
- **A "go deeper" line** for each work appears under your first impression, once it is written. The locked prompt is unchanged.

### Stickers (supersedes Round 4 and Round 6 gilding)
- A first impression **draws one sticker from that maker's set of six**, never a repeat. Sets in the prototype: Barrett Browning, Chesterton, Hammershoi (six each, every one tied to a real work or fact).
- **The day's sticker is the one you drew last, carrying a small check.** Tap another of today's to move it. It settles at midnight; no deadline, no counting strip, no "2/5".
- **Gilding is a free finish**, not a reward: gild anything you own from its card in the profile, and take it off again.
- The profile now shows **maker shelves**: only makers you have met, six slots each, undrawn slots grey and unnamed, no totals.

### The Notebook (Notebook Studio round 2, now live)
- **One notebook, four ways in plus an index.** Page (the default), Board, Questions, Today, Index. Every entry is a commonplace page: up to three kept lines on top, writing below, optionally hanging on a question.
- Tray of kept lines grouped by where they came from; "Add a line" for your own; art from a muse sits small at the top of a page with "look closer".
- Board is the corkboard of every kept line; choose up to three and "write under these". Questions keep a shelf with the last line on each, a "Still breathing?" nudge after a fortnight, a thread of pages, and "this has become something". Index sorts by date, source or question, with search.
- **Phone is Pocket only**: catch a line, it waits on the desk. This answers the desktop-only worry against pillar 2.
- The old season dial, journal/scrapbook/idea-board split is retired. **Rule of life survives** as a quiet tab at the end of the notebook bar.
- Keeping from a muse now offers "keep the line" or "write a page under it" instead of journal/scrapbook/board.

## Round 10 (25 Sept 2026): the copy file, and the publish folder
- **Every word of the site frame now lives in `copy.js`**, a plain `key: value` list you edit like a text file. About 240 lines, grouped by where they appear: the frame, the hero, the Muse page, reading others, links, stickers, the Notebook (tray, page, board, questions, today, index, Pocket), the profile, the whole Why page, the small messages at the bottom of the screen, and the quick pen.
- Editing a line and reloading changes the site. **Deleting a line, or mistyping a key, falls back to the built-in wording** — the page cannot break from a copy edit. Whole-line `#` comments are ignored; a backtick is the one character the file must not contain.
- Left out on purpose: the day's three works and their stories (they change daily and live in `index.html`), and the Settings panel's option names.
- **The publish folder is `Desktop\Poeisis\publish\`**: `index.html`, `copy.js`, `README.md`, `LICENSE`, `404.html`, `.nojekyll`. That folder, and nothing else, is what goes to GitHub. The earlier `poiesis-site` folder is superseded and can be deleted.
- Version 10 of the live artifact carries `copy.js` as a companion file, so the artifact and the GitHub copy read the same words.
- Also in this round (Round 9 continued): the day's word sits big on the left with the three works on the right, one line, on both desktop and phone; it no longer waits for all three impressions. "N others wrote today" moved beside the work's title. The poem is 28 lines (Barrett Browning); Hopkins and Keats are now whole sonnets at 14 lines each, which is the whole poem rather than the 25-line minimum.
